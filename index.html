Tech Yuva is a student-led innovation guild from New Delhi — empowering the next generation of builders through AI, Web3, production systems, and startup culture. We don't just teach. We deploy alongside our builders.

Zero fees. Real projects. Actual deployment.

What We Do

Five pillars.
One mission.

Build

Scalable APIs, full-stack architectures, production backends. No toy projects — real systems that handle real users.

02

Learn

03

Connect

500+ builders, corporate tech talks, open-source collaboration. A network of people who build, not just talk.

04

Compete

36-hour hackathons, coding sprints, DropHack events. Solve unknown problems under real pressure.

05

Innovate

PitchCraft Accelerator — angel reviews, funding connections, mentorship, and cloud credits to launch your startup.
